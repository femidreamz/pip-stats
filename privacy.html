Pip Stats Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Who this covers

Pip Stats is used only by the owner of the TikTok account @pipsaysbirthday. It does not collect information from anyone else.

2. What we read from TikTok

With the owner's permission, through TikTok's official API, Pip Stats reads:

  • basic profile information (account ID, display name, avatar);
  • account statistics (follower, following, like and video counts);
  • the account's own public videos and their statistics (views, likes, comments and shares).

3. How it is used

The information is used only to show the owner which of their own videos perform best, on a private dashboard. It is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers or used for any other purpose.

4. Where it is kept

The statistics and the TikTok access token are stored on the owner's private server. Access tokens are never published or shared.

5. How long it is kept, and deleting it

Statistics are kept for as long as the dashboard is used. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings under Security > Apps and services; the stored token and statistics are then deleted.

6. Children

Pip Stats is not directed to children and does not collect information from them.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Questions can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/femidreamz/pip-stats/issues.
