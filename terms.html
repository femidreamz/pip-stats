Pip Stats Terms of Service

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. What Pip Stats is

Pip Stats is a private tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @pipsaysbirthday. It reads that account's own video statistics through TikTok's official API and shows them on the owner's private dashboard.

2. Who may use it

Pip Stats is not offered to the public. Only the account owner connects their TikTok account, and they can disconnect it at any time in TikTok's settings under Security > Apps and services.

3. What it does and does not do

Pip Stats only reads basic profile information, account statistics and the list of the account's own videos with their statistics. It does not post, edit, delete, comment, like, follow or send messages on TikTok.

4. TikTok's terms

Use of Pip Stats is also subject to TikTok's Terms of Service and TikTok's Developer Terms. Pip Stats is not made, endorsed or supported by TikTok.

5. No warranty

Pip Stats is provided as is, without any warranty. Statistics come from TikTok and may be delayed or incomplete.
