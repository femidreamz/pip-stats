Pip Stats

Pip Stats is a small private tool for the owner of the TikTok account @pipsaysbirthday. It reads that account's own public video statistics (views, likes, comments and shares) through TikTok's official API and shows them on the owner's private dashboard, so the owner can see which birthday videos people enjoy most.

It does not post, comment, follow, message or change anything on TikTok, and it is not offered to other users.
